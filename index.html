01About

My name is Subhajit Chattopadhyay, and I am currently a Research Scholar in the School of Mechanical Sciences at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bhubaneswar. I am deeply involved in advanced research related to materials engineering, with a particular focus on metallic composites, high-entropy alloys, Mg-based biomaterials, and laser surface texturing.
